AlphaZero vs. an Olympiad Champion: Self-Taught Hex on a Laptop

Python, JAX, Flax

  • Built AlphaZero in JAX (batched MCTS, policy/value network, self-play) and let it teach itself Hex from zero on a MacBook Air: no human games, no GPU. Inspired by Andy Jones's Scaling Scaling Laws with Board Games.
  • Put it up against MoHex 2.0, a Computer Olympiad champion, with MoHex's exact solver as referee: proven perfect on 3x3, wins every 5x5 opening it should, and holds the win in 99.2% of solver-checked 7x7 positions.
  • Tracked down why it kept losing four 5x5 openings no matter how long it trained (self-play never visited them); random first moves took it from 21/25 to a perfect 25/25.

Mixture-of-Experts Layer for Named Entity Recognition

Python, PyTorch, HuggingFace

  • Built a Mixture-of-Experts layer from scratch (4 expert MLPs, top-2 gating, load-balancing loss) and added it to a BiLSTM tagger with GloVe, character-CNN and casing features.
  • Tested on CoNLL-2003 over 3 seeds against a plain BiLSTM and a parameter-matched dense-FFN control. The balanced MoE matched the baseline (89.3 ± 0.4 vs 89.4 ± 0.3 entity-F1) and beat the unbalanced MoE by about 0.9 F1, with expert usage spread evenly (17–33% per expert).
