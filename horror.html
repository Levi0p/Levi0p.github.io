Horror

just my opinion obv, the ratings below are based entirely on my personal preferences

  • I value storytelling and atmosphere much, much more than random ass jumpscares
  • I also prefer newer movies over older ones (for this list, anything released in 2005 or earlier counts as old)
  • I'm primarily interested in actual horror horror, rather than zombie horror or psychological horror, although I'll make exceptions for psychological horror if it's really good
  • I also judge movies by how much they actually scare me rather than how much they merely entertain or disturb me, for me frightened ≥ disturbed > entertained, i generally prefer supernatural, possession, occult and folk horror over psychological horror, bonus points go to movies that still give me the creeps even into adulthood
  • I also tend to rate movies higher if they have bleak endings rather than ones where everyone lives happily ever after, i think a bleak ending can add a lot to a story and make the horror linger long after the movie ends

if you are someone who has similar preferences to mine, then i think you should give this list a try
